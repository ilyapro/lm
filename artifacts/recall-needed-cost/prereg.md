## Selection and split

The private packet has nine cases: six development cases and three holdout cases. Development includes the verified supervisor large-carrier query (the target carrier had 56,959 content characters in the observation), two ordinary short project facts, a separate instruction in the last 2% of a long evidence group, a historical wrong claim with its explicit correction, and an impossible missing-knowledge control. Holdout includes one different short fact, one instruction in a different long group, and a different impossible missing-knowledge control. Holdout query strings, target IDs, and answer predicates stay in the private packet until the candidate implementation and its tuning decisions are fixed. The public synthetic corpus has the same families, plus separate holdout nodes.

Selection used the frozen snapshot only, before candidate measurement. The known carrier is pinned from the prior supervisor observation. Long instruction groups were selected from active concept rows over 5,000 characters with applicable imperatives beyond 65% of content, then hand checked for the precise task condition. The correction group was selected because it records both an old false assertion and a current correction. Short facts were chosen by ascending SHA-256 of `recall-needed-cost-v1:` plus node ID among active `project:lm` trace rows of 80–600 characters that are ASCII, mention `src/living_memory/`, have a first sentence of 35–500 characters and at least five words, and are not source traces of the chosen long groups. The first two eligible short records went to development; the third went to holdout. The short questions were authored from the selected topic without copying the answer clause. Missing-knowledge questions use distinct nonsensical sentinels absent from the snapshot.

The packet records for each case `id`, `split`, `family`, `query`, `scope` (nullable for broad recall), `max_results`, and an `oracle` with `required_source_ids`, `must_contain`, `must_not_assert`, and `absence_expected`. Source IDs, full questions and answer clauses are private. Development and holdout have disjoint target IDs and distinct questions. No holdout result may be used to tune selection, delivery, ranking, or the oracle; if holdout fails, report the failure without replacing a case.

## Independent reader and cost rule

The oracle reads the frozen packet and snapshot, not delivery code, to decide whether the accumulated delivered text supplies each required current claim. Normalize only whitespace for literal clause checks. A required source ID is a provenance requirement, not an answer by itself: the corresponding required clause must be present in the response or in a later lookup. For a correction, the newer claim and explicit negation of the old rule must both be present; an obsolete sentence quoted as false in source evidence is not itself a violation. `must_not_assert` forbids presenting that old sentence as current. A missing-knowledge control succeeds only when the reader states that the snapshot does not establish an answer, never by inventing one. If a positive case cannot reach the required claims, report it as insufficient rather than silently awarding a cost win. The synthetic correction and buried-instruction cases must fail when their required clauses are deliberately removed.

Run the accepted baseline and candidate on separate fresh copies of the **same snapshot bytes**, in the same frozen case order, with identical recall arguments. Include every recall response character. If required knowledge is not yet delivered and recall supplies a source reference, perform the minimal relevant `memory_lookup` calls required for the oracle, counting each complete lookup response and latency. Stop when the oracle is sufficient, or record an explicit insufficiency after reachable relevant sources are exhausted. An already sufficient short response incurs no lookup. Count JSON response text once, not duplicated MCP transport representations. Report per case and aggregate characters, recall calls, lookup calls, elapsed time, sufficiency, and ordered retrieval IDs. Report any change in ranking separately from presentation savings. Include development and holdout separately in the result and do not infer a general benefit from the known carrier alone.

The private cases and snapshot may be read locally by the later runner and tests, but only synthetic fixture contents, this selection method, hashes, and aggregate evidence may enter git. If a source node, query, oracle clause, split, or snapshot changes, mint a new preregistration with new hashes and do not call the old and new packets paired.
